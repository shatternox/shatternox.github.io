    Read the turns

    Hold the whole cube steady. Each letter tells you which outer layer to turn. Clockwise always means as if you were looking directly at that face, even for the back or bottom.

    U Top
    R Right
    F Front
    D Bottom
    L Left
    B Back

    R is a 90° clockwise turn. R′ is a 90° counterclockwise turn. R2 is 180° in either direction.

    The camera can move without changing what U, R, or F mean. The center colors under the cube tell you how to hold your physical cube.
