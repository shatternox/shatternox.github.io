Privacy Policy

YouTube Channel Publisher is a private tool run by Ardian Danny. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

What it does

The tool signs in only to our own YouTube channel, through Google OAuth, to upload our own videos and read our channel's statistics and comments.

Data

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone else.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on our own computer and is never shared.
  • Data from Google APIs is used only to run our own channel, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • The tool uses the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
